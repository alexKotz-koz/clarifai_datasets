# Synthetic PI Feedback

> Two synthetically generated datasets were created using our custom [Synthetic Feedback Generation Pipeline](https://github.com/alexKotz-koz/Synthetic-Feedback-Generation-Pipeline/blob/master/README.md). The first dataset leveraged 50 clincian personas to respond to 3 usability questions on a prototype healthcare application called PI. PI is a prototype of a patient intake application, to collect healthcare patient reported outcomes and other screeners/forms from a patient. We generated four wireframes of the PI application and a short text narrative describing the app at a high level. We sent these to the LLMs as context, in addition to a given persona and tasked the LLMs to respond to 3 questions on the wireframes for version 1 and 1 question on the wireframes on version 2.
